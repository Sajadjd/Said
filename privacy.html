Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This application processes video content and related information only to provide the requested video processing and publishing workflow.

Information We Process

Depending on how the service is used, we may process video files, video titles, hashtags, Telegram user identifiers, Google Drive file information, and TikTok authorization information.

How We Use Information

We use this information to download, process, store temporarily, upload, and publish videos to the TikTok account authorized by the user.

Third-Party Services

Videos and related information may be transferred to Telegram, Google Drive, TikTok, and video processing providers when required to complete the requested workflow.

Data Retention

Temporary video files are deleted when they are no longer required for processing. Authorization tokens are stored securely and are not intentionally shared with other users.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the services required to perform the workflow requested by the user.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the application owner through the account used to operate the service.
